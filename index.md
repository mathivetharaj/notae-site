Notae turns speech into clean text and well-structured AI prompts. Tap on, speak, tap off, and get your words in the mode you chose, inserted where you're typing or ready to paste into ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor or Slack.

- Works on iPhone and iPad, with the Notae Keyboard for any app.
- Transcribes on your device. Writes prompts on your device with Apple Intelligence, or with Claude or OpenAI using your own key.
- No account, no ads, no analytics.
