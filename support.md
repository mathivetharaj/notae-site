Need help with Notae? Start here. If your question isn't answered, [open an issue](https://github.com/mathivetharaj/notae-site/issues/new) and describe what happened. Please don't include API keys or private dictations.

## Getting started

1. Open Notae, allow the microphone and speech recognition, and let the speech model download.
2. Pick a mode (for example **Coding task** or **Professional email**), tap the record button, speak, and tap again.
3. Your text appears in that mode and is copied, ready to paste.

## Using the Notae Keyboard

1. Go to **Settings › General › Keyboard › Keyboards › Add New Keyboard** and choose **Notae**.
2. Tap **Notae** in that list and turn on **Allow Full Access**. Notae uses it only to talk to the Notae app on your device; it never logs what you type.
3. In any app, hold or tap the globe key to switch to Notae.
4. Tap the dictation button. The first time, Notae opens to start a keyboard session. Go back to your app with the **‹ back** link at the top left, or by swiping along the bottom edge.
5. Tap the button again to start, speak, and tap to stop. The text is inserted where you're typing. **Undo** removes it; **Insert raw** puts in exactly what you said.

A session stays ready for 5 minutes of inactivity (you can change this in Settings). The orange microphone indicator shows while a session is on; Notae only transcribes between your taps.

## Starting Notae without opening it

- **Control Center:** add the **Notae Keyboard** control to start or end a keyboard session, or **Dictate** to start recording.
- **Siri and Shortcuts:** say "Dictate a prompt with Notae".
- **Action Button or Back Tap:** assign the Dictate a prompt shortcut (Back Tap is in **Settings › Accessibility › Touch**).

## AI engines

- **On-device (Apple Intelligence):** private and works offline, on devices with Apple Intelligence turned on.
- **Claude or OpenAI:** add your own API key in **Settings › AI engine**. Requests go straight from your device to the provider and are billed to your account.
- **Clean transcript:** always available, no AI model. Removes filler words and fixes punctuation.

Notae always shows which engine wrote each result.

## Transcription

- **This device (default):** private and offline. Pick your accent under **Settings › Transcription › Accent** if it differs from your region.
- **ElevenLabs Scribe or OpenAI:** for the best accuracy with accents and technical terms, add your own key and choose it under **Settings › Transcription › Transcribe with**. Words still appear live as you speak; when you stop, the audio goes to that service and its transcript replaces the live one. If it can't be reached, Notae uses the on-device transcript.
- **Mixing languages:** if you mix another language into English, choose what happens under **Settings › Transcription › Other languages**: keep those words as spoken, translate them to English, or write them in English letters. Translation and English letters need an AI mode (not Clean transcript).

## Troubleshooting

- **"Speech model not ready":** keep Notae open on Wi-Fi until the download finishes.
- **Prompts are only clean transcripts:** Apple Intelligence is off or unavailable on this device. Turn it on in **Settings › Apple Intelligence & Siri**, or add a Claude or OpenAI key.
- **"Too quiet" or "Noisy here":** move closer to the microphone, or tap **Mic mode** and choose **Voice Isolation**.
- **The keyboard says "Turn on Full Access":** see step 2 above.
- **Nothing was inserted:** Notae never inserts anything when it heard nothing. Try again a little closer to the microphone.
